# Extra ideas — FINAL CHOSEN LIST (21 features)

These are all approved and will be built into the code.

| # | Idea | What it does |
|---|------|--------------|
| 1 | **Hunger slider** | Guest drags "just a snack" -> "starving". AI adjusts portion advice. |
| 2 | **Weather & time aware** | Cold rainy night -> AI leans to soup and hot drinks. Hot noon -> light and cold. Happens automatically. |
| 3 | **Remember me everywhere** | Guest saves allergies and taste once. Next restaurant on the platform already knows them. |
| 4 | **One-tap feedback** | After eating: "How was it?" AI learns, owner sees which dish disappoints. |
| 5 | **Split the bill** | Whole table scans the same QR, each person orders under their name, bill splits automatically. |
| 6 | **Voice ordering** | Guest speaks instead of typing. Good for older guests. |
| 7 | **Honest wait time** | AI says "that one is 25 min, this one is 10 if you're in a hurry". |
| 8 | **Allergy warning on kitchen ticket** | Guest allergies print in red on the kitchen order. |
| 9 | **Daily calorie budget** | "I already ate 1200 today, limit 2000" -> AI fits dinner into what's left. |
| 10 | **Tourist mode** | Whole menu + AI in the tourist's language, explains local dishes. |
| 11 | **Polite upsell** | AI offers dessert/coffee once, only after the main order, never pushy. |
| 12 | **Order for later** | "Bring dessert in 20 min". Kitchen ticket shows the timing. |
| 13 | **Weather drink pairing** | AI proposes the right drink for the weather even if guest only ordered food. |
| 14 | **Kids mode** | "Child, 6 years old" -> only small, non-spicy, safe dishes with a playful UI. |
| 15 | **Wait-time quiz** | While food cooks, a small quiz about the cuisine. Just for fun, no discount. |
| 16 | **Fasting / religious mode** | Ramadan iftar timing, Lent (no meat), etc. AI respects it automatically by date. |
| 17 | **Hydration reminder** | For athletes: AI reminds to add water and how much for their body size. |
| 18 | **Post-gym timing** | "I just trained" -> AI prioritises fast protein + carbs and explains why. |
| 19 | **Leftover warning** | If the order is too much for the hunger level, AI politely says so. |
| 20 | **Kitchen busy signal** | When many orders are queued, guests see a warning and AI suggests faster dishes. |
| 21 | **Goal tracking across visits** | Athlete profile keeps daily protein/calorie totals across all restaurants on the platform. |
